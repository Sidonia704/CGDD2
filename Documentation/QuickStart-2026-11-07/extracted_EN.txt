MAKE FIRE BUTTONS WORK
In WEAPON GROUPS, drag a weapon to a group and
release to assign it. A group click only shows its members.
New/default groups: 1 = direct guns / left mouse, 2 =
missiles / right mouse. Saved ships may use different
bindings.

YOUR MISSION
Defeat all 6 waves and remaining enemies. The final wave
brings the t8 flagship + escorts. Your core being destroyed
ends the sortie. Repair the design and redeploy.
3  READ THE FIGHT
RED TARGETS Radar contacts; F selects your main target.
SHIELD Absorbs hits. It recovers using energy.
ENERGY Powers propulsion, weapons and shield recovery.
WEAPON CELLS Bright = ready. Dim / a number =
unavailable or reloading.
HEAT Gatling orange heat rises during fire. After
overheating, cool to empty to fire again.
AIM, FIRE, RECOVER
Beams: aim at the ship. Projectile guns: use the red lead
marker for moving targets. Slight assistance corrects
mounting offset only.
Regular missiles: F-lock a target, wait for missile
acquisition, then hold the missile group's fire button.
Reloading missiles must acquire again.
VLS: keep radar-marked enemies in view for 4 seconds,
then fire its group. It splits the magazine across targets;
each unit reloads independently.
Low energy? Release boost and stop firing briefly. Shield
recovery gets power first, then propulsion, then
weapons. Boost raises the speed limit; releasing it
restores the normal limit.
